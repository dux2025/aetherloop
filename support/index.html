Popular Help Topics

Step-by-step guidance for managing Revive on your iOS device

Account & Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You can delete individual dreams or your entire account at any time.

  • 1 Open Revive on your iPhone and go to Profile / Settings.
  • 2 Tap Account and select Delete Account.
  • 3 Or email reviveappsupport@gmail.com with your user email/ID. We delete or de-identify personal data within 30 days.

Managing Subscriptions & Billing

All purchases and renewals are securely processed by Apple via your Apple ID.

  • 1 Open iPhone Settings and tap your Apple ID name at the top.
  • 2 Tap Subscriptions, then select Revive.
  • 3 Tap Cancel Subscription at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid auto-renewal charges.

Microphone & Permissions

Revive allows you to speak your dream immediately upon waking for voice-to-text transcription.

  • 1 Microphone is strictly used when you tap the record button to capture a dream.
  • 2 Revive never listens in the background or collects unsolicited audio.
  • 3 You can toggle permissions anytime in iPhone Settings > Revive > Microphone.
