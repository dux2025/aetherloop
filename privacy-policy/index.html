Reverie — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 Last updated: October 12, 2026 Data Controller: Reverie Contact: reverieappsupport@gmail.com

1. Introduction #

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Reverie (“Reverie,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you.

This Policy applies to the Reverie iOS application and its related services (collectively, the “Service”).

Data controller: Reverie

Contact email: reverieappsupport@gmail.com

By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these practices, please do not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect #

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Dream content: Text you enter and audio you record to describe your dreams, along with any notes, tags, or mood entries you add.
  • Account information: Your email address, sign-in identifier (such as Sign in with Apple), and user ID, depending on how you create your account.
  • Support communications: Information and messages you send when contacting our support team.

2.2 Information Generated by the Service

  • AI-generated content: Dream interpretations, images, videos, and pattern or insight summaries generated from your dream content.
  • Dream archive and insights: Your saved dreams and recurring symbols, themes, and emotions identified across your dream history.

2.3 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Features used, screens viewed, and interactions with the app, collected through analytics tools.
  • Device and diagnostic data: Device model, iOS version, app version, language, crash logs, and other diagnostic information.
  • Purchase information: Subscription status and transaction identifiers provided through Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details.

2.4 Device Permissions

Reverie uses the following optional device permissions:

Microphone
We request microphone access when you choose to record a dream. Your recording is transcribed into text so that we can provide dream interpretations and related features. Recordings are stored in your dream archive until you delete them.

Reverie does not use the microphone to listen in the background. If you decline microphone access, you can continue using the app by typing your dreams.

Notifications
If you grant notification permission, Reverie may send reminders to record your dreams at your chosen time and notify you when an interpretation, image, or video is ready.

We store your notification preferences and may store a push notification token associated with your account to deliver these notifications. Notifications are not used for advertising. You can decline or disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

Reverie does not knowingly collect precise location data, access your contacts, or collect photos from your device under the practices described in this Policy.

2.5 Sensitive Content in Dreams

Dreams may contain personal or sensitive information about health, relationships, beliefs, emotions, or other aspects of your life.

We do not ask you to provide health information, and we do not treat dream content as medical records. We process dream content to provide the Service described in this Privacy Policy.

Please avoid including information you do not want processed by our Service or its third-party providers.

3. How We Use Information #

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide dream interpretations and generate images, videos, and insights.
  • Store and manage your dream archive and account.
  • Transcribe voice recordings into text.
  • Process subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Service.
  • Prevent abuse and moderate generated content.
  • Diagnose crashes, troubleshoot technical problems, and improve app performance.
  • Understand app usage through analytics.
  • Respond to support requests and send service-related communications.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3.1 AI Processing and Third-Party Providers

To provide AI-powered interpretations, images, videos, and voice transcription, we send the information necessary to fulfil your request to third-party service providers.

Depending on the feature you use, this may include your dream text, a transcription of your voice recording, or other information needed to generate the requested output.

These providers may include AI language-model, speech-transcription, image-generation, video-generation, cloud-hosting, analytics, and diagnostic services.

We use service providers acting on our behalf and require appropriate contractual restrictions on their use of personal information, where applicable.

For information about the specific providers used by Reverie, contact us at reverieappsupport@gmail.com.

3.2 AI Model Training

Our AI Model Training Commitment:

We do not use your dream content to train our own AI models or intentionally submit it for training third-party AI models.

3.3 Analytics and Diagnostics

We use analytics and crash-reporting services to understand how the app is used, identify errors, and improve performance.

We do not use your dream content for advertising, sell your personal information, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3.4 AI Limitations

AI-generated interpretations, images, videos, and insights may be inaccurate, incomplete, unexpected, or misleading.

Disclaimer: Reverie is intended for personal reflection and entertainment. It does not provide medical, psychological, diagnostic, or therapeutic advice. Do not rely on AI-generated interpretations as a substitute for professional advice.

5. Sharing Your Information #

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with the following categories of recipients when necessary:

  • Service providers: Cloud hosting, AI processing, voice transcription, analytics, crash reporting, and customer support providers acting on our behalf.
  • Apple: For purchases, subscriptions, authentication, and related platform services, where applicable.
  • Legal and safety authorities: When required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or enforce our Terms and Conditions.
  • Business successors: In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards.

We share only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Private by Default: Your dreams are private by default. They are not visible to other users unless you explicitly choose to share them through a feature that allows sharing.

6. Data Retention and Deletion #

6.1 Retention

We retain your dreams and generated content while your account remains active, unless you delete them earlier or a different retention period is required by law.

We retain analytics and diagnostic data for up to 24 months.

Following deletion, backup copies are scheduled to be purged within 90 days, subject to applicable legal requirements and technical limitations.

6.2 Deleting Individual Dreams

You can delete individual dreams from your archive through the app, where the relevant feature is available.

6.3 Deleting Your Account

You can request deletion of your account and associated data through the account-deletion feature in the app or by emailing reverieappsupport@gmail.com.

We delete or de-identify personal information within 30 days of a valid deletion request, except where retention is required or permitted by applicable law.

Some information may remain in backups until those backups are purged in accordance with our retention practices. Information retained for legal or security purposes will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Data Security #

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit and at rest and restrictions on internal access.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Please keep your device and account credentials secure and avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in your dreams.

8. Your Privacy Rights #

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Request a copy or export of your information, where applicable.
  • Object to or request restrictions on certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

You can exercise applicable rights through the app or by contacting reverieappsupport@gmail.com.

We will respond within the time required by applicable law and may request information necessary to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

8.1 EEA and UK Users

Where applicable, we process personal information on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: To provide the Service you request.
  • Legitimate interests: To secure, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Service, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: Where required, including for certain optional permissions or processing activities.
  • Legal obligation: To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Where required, you may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

8.2 California and Other US State Residents

Depending on applicable state law, you may have rights to know, access, correct, or delete personal information and to exercise other privacy rights.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising under the practices described in this Policy. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

To submit a request, contact reverieappsupport@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy #

Reverie is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 where applicable law requires a higher age threshold.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact reverieappsupport@gmail.com so we can review the situation and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

10. International Data Transfers #

Your information may be processed or stored in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States, depending on the service providers we use.

Where required by applicable law, we implement appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy #

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app, data practices, service providers, or legal requirements.

If we make material changes, we will provide notice through the app or by email where appropriate and required by law.

We will update the effective date at the top of this page when a revised Policy takes effect.
